Skin Education

Skin Health Awareness & Care

Empowering you with essential dermatologist insights, common skin/hair concerns, and preventative measures.

Diseases & Prevention

Common Skin Concerns

  • Acne Vulgaris: Prevent by cleansing twice daily with salicylic acid wash, avoiding picking, and using non-comedogenic cosmetics.
  • Psoriasis & Eczema: Manage flare-ups by moisturizing daily with ceramide creams, avoiding hot showers, and using gentle, fragrance-free body washes.
Hair & Scalp Care

Hair & Scalp Concerns

  • Hair Fall & Alopecia: Prevent by consuming a balanced diet rich in iron, zinc, and protein, avoiding tight hairstyles, and minimizing heat-styling.
  • Dandruff & Itchiness: Prevent by washing scalp with anti-dandruff shampoo (containing zinc pyrithione or ketoconazole) and avoiding heavy hair oils.
Sun Safety

UV Protection Guide

  • Broad-Spectrum Sunscreen: Apply SPF 30+ daily. Reapply every 2 hours when outdoors. This prevents premature aging and reduces skin cancer risk.
  • Physical UV Protection: Wear protective UV-blocking sunglasses, wide-brimmed hats, and seek shade during peak sunlight (10:00 AM - 04:00 PM).
Daily Wellness

Daily Skin Wellness

  • Skin Barrier Repair: Lock in hydration using hyaluronic acid and squalane. Avoid over-exfoliating with harsh physical scrubs.
  • Healthy Habits: Stay hydrated (2L+ water daily), prioritize 8 hours of sleep, and eat antioxidant-rich foods to promote natural radiance.

When to Consult a Dermatologist

While preventative skincare is highly beneficial, self-care alone may not be sufficient for clinical conditions. Please consult our dermatologist if you experience persistent irritation, worsening rashes, changes in moles, or chronic skin/hair concerns that do not improve.
